PMCS Interval Guidelines

×

PMCS involves checks at various intervals to ensure vehicle readiness and safety:

Before
Before Operation Checks

Performed before moving the vehicle. Identify safety hazards and mission-critical defects before operation begins.

During
During Operation Checks

Monitor gauges, listen for unusual sounds, and observe vehicle performance during operation.

After
After Operation Checks

Conducted after operation. Check for new leaks, damage, and prepare for next operation.

Weekly
Weekly Checks

Performed once every 7 days. More detailed inspections to prevent breakdowns.

Monthly
Monthly Checks

Conducted once every 30 days. Comprehensive inspections for long-term vehicle health.

Note: Always refer to TM 9-2320-365-10 for complete PMCS requirements.
