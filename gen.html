PMCS Interval Guidelines

×

Generator PMCS involves checks at various intervals to ensure operational readiness:

Before
Before Operation Checks

Performed before starting the generator. Identify fuel, oil, and electrical issues before operation begins.

During
During Operation Checks

Monitor voltage, frequency, temperature, and load while the generator is running.

After
After Operation Checks

Performed after shutdown. Check for new leaks, fuel level, cable condition, and secure all panels.

Weekly
Weekly Checks

More detailed inspection of batteries, fuel filters, belts, and cleaning requirements.

Monthly
Monthly Checks

Comprehensive service including oil change, fuel filter replacement, load test, and full system inspection.

Note: Always refer to the applicable TM for complete PMCS requirements and hour-based service intervals.
