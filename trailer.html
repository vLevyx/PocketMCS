PMCS Interval Guidelines

×

Trailer PMCS involves checks at various intervals to ensure safe towing and operational readiness:

Before
Before Operation Checks

Performed before hitching and towing. Inspect coupling, tires, brakes, lighting, and cargo securing hardware before any movement.

During
During Operation Checks

Monitor trailer tracking, stability, and lighting while towing. Watch for sway, unusual noise, or signs of tire failure.

After
After Operation Checks

Performed after unhitching. Check for new damage, fluid leaks, tire condition, and brake temperature.

Weekly
Weekly Checks

More detailed inspection of wheel bearings, suspension, brake lines, lunette lubrication, and wiring harness.

Monthly
Monthly Checks

Comprehensive service including tire rotation, torque check, full brake inspection, frame inspection, and coupling wear measurement.

Note: Always refer to TM 9-2330-392-13&P for complete PMCS requirements and service intervals.
